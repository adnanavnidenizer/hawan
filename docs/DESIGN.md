# HAWAN design direction

Preserve the root V1 identity: natural wood photography, warm stone and cream tones, Cormorant Garamond headings, restrained DM Sans copy, generous negative space and quiet interactions. Use existing assets and avoid invented product variants, prices or availability. Design new pages as part of the same site, with localized navigation and equivalent EN/TR routes.

Preview architecture: /en/home, /en/gallery, /en/shop and /tr/ana-sayfa, /tr/galeri, /tr/magaza. Main production must remain unchanged until the user approves merging.

All HAWAN mortars have three legs. Preserve photographed leg geometry, recessed visible legs, negative spaces and reflections in all product cutouts. Never invent hidden legs or erase visible wooden parts as background.
