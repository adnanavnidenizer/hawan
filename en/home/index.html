Function inspires the wood, and the wood inspires the design. With designs that prioritize functionality and are shaped by the natural grain of the wood, HAWAN offers mortars and pestles that enrich their surroundings through their presence and add value to life through their use.

Texture

A Serene
Touch

Appealing to the tactile senses as much as to the eye, HAWAN encourages you to slow down and feel through its exceptionally smooth texture. Touching its silky surface is soothing. Every grinding process becomes a calming ritual.

Utility

Refined Details
That Enhance Use

With thoughtful touches that elevate the use of the mortar and pestle, HAWAN offers a complete grinding experience. Its interior form keeps the ingredients inside the bowl while grinding, allowing you to control their fineness with ease. Its three-legged design prevents it from tipping or wobbling.

The Process

Crafted for You,
Finished by Hand.

Every piece is an intimate collaboration. We do not mass produce; we craft specifically to order, marrying quiet minimalism with natural warmth for your personal space.

01

Material Selection

Choose your medium. From the deep, grounding tones of Black Walnut to the clean, minimalist grain of White Ash. Sustainably sourced, carefully selected.

02

Organic Design

Select your dimensions. Our designs are guided by natural simplicity—stripping away the unnecessary to reveal pure, flowing shapes that perfectly balance function and beauty.

03

Tactile Finish

Each object is carefully shaped, then sanded and finished by hand, and sealed with food-safe natural oils to enhance the wood's inherent character and feel.

"I carved the first HAWAN entirely by hand about ten years ago, following an unsuccessful attempt at woodturning. Since then, each object has taught me that the wood itself is part of the design. Knots, grain patterns and unexpected marks often change the direction of the process; in truth, we design each piece together."

MEET THE DESIGNER

Adnan Avni

Adnan Avni was born in Istanbul in 1962. In 1985, he graduated from the Department of Industrial Design and Interior Architecture at Mimar Sinan University’s Faculty of Fine Arts. Alongside graphic design and illustration, he worked in the design and production of stands and displays for points of sale.

Following the exhibition he held at the Çankaya Municipality Zülfü Livaneli Cultural Centre in June 2024, he settled in Gökçeyurt, a village in Ankara’s Mamak district. He continues his work in painting and woodcarving in the village.
